Спроба 2 · How honey is produced

The diagram below shows how honey is produced, from the collection of nectar by bees to the final packaged product. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Єдина підказка · фази й акториBody 1 = bees (етапи 1–4, тут АКТИВ legal: bees collect, store, fan — але матеріал бере верх у пасиві: cells are sealed). Body 2 = beekeeper (5–8, суто пасив: frames are removed…). Місток: «In the second phase…». Заверши фразою «ready for sale».
ІнструкціяПиши всю відповідь одразу (§1–§4, 150+ слів). Без таймера. Після чек-листа відкриється модель.
0 слів (мінімум 150)

Чек-лист перед моделлю

§1 The diagram illustrates how honey is produced, from the collection of nectar to the final packaged product.

§2 Overall, the process is linear and falls into two phases: production inside the hive, carried out by the bees, followed by harvesting by the beekeeper. It begins with nectar collection and concludes with filtering and jarring.

§3 In the first phase, worker bees collect nectar from flowers and store it in the cells of the hive. The bees then fan their wings, which causes excess water to evaporate. Once the honey has thickened, the cells are sealed with wax.

§4 In the second phase, the frames are removed from the hive, after the bees have been calmed with smoke. The wax seals are then scraped off, and the frames are spun in a centrifuge, which extracts the honey. Finally, it is filtered and poured into jars, ready for sale.

Порівняй рішення: актив там, де діють бджоли, пасив — у фазі бджоляра? «once the honey has thickened» — perfect? Два актори названі в overview?

Спроба 3 · How four types of tea are produced · exam conditions

The diagram below shows how four types of tea — white, green, oolong and black — are produced from the leaves of the same plant. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Чек-лист перед моделлю (усі 8 — чесно)

§1 The diagram illustrates how four distinct types of tea — white, green, oolong and black — are produced from the leaves of the same plant.

§2 Overall, all four teas share the same initial stages of plucking and withering, after which the process branches into four separate routes, depending on how much the leaves are oxidised.

§3 Initially, the young leaves are plucked by hand and are withered to reduce their moisture content. At this point, the process divides. White tea undergoes the least processing: the leaves are simply dried. Green tea leaves are first steamed or pan-fired to prevent oxidation, before being rolled and dried.

§4 Oolong and black tea follow longer routes. Oolong leaves are bruised and allowed to oxidise partially — between 10 and 70 per cent — before they are rolled and dried. Black tea, meanwhile, is crushed and fully oxidised, which gives the leaves their dark colour; they are then dried and packed, ready for sale.

Порівняй рішення: стовбур описаний ОДИН раз? Точка розгалуження названа? Гілки за мірою окислення? «by contrast / meanwhile» між гілками?
